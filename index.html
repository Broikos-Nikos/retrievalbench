Method

The task is self retrieval: a sentence is taken out of an article and used as the query, so the passage that contains it is the right answer by construction and nothing had to be labelled. It is a weak task on purpose, because it flatters every method, so a difference that survives it is real. Every query is asked twice, once as the sentence and once as its longest words in the order they appear, which is closer to what a person types. The two families give the same answer.

A result is right when it holds the text somebody asked for, measured as the fraction of the query's characters inside the returned chunk. Scoring by "the chunk the sentence started in" was the first rule written, and it is unfair to any policy whose chunks overlap: it put the overlap policy at 0.823 against 0.938 for fixed, a difference that was entirely an artefact of the rule.
